About

A Little About Dorpon

দর্পণ – Dorpon is a photography and cinematography brand based in Rajbari, Bangladesh, serving Rajbari, Faridpur and Dhaka. We focus on capturing meaningful moments and turning them into timeless visual stories.

What services does Dorpon offer?

Dorpon offers photography and cinematography for weddings, holud ceremonies, portraits and events in Rajbari, Faridpur and Dhaka, Bangladesh.

Where is Dorpon based?

Dorpon is based in Rajbari, Bangladesh, and serves Rajbari, Faridpur and Dhaka. It is a service-area business with no walk-in office, and travels to clients for shoots.

How can I book Dorpon?

Fill in the booking and inquiry form on this page, message Dorpon on WhatsApp, or call 01740-744170.

How can I contact Dorpon?

Call or WhatsApp 01740-744170, email contact@rajinmd.com, or use the booking form on this page.

Can I see Dorpon's previous work before booking?

Yes. The portfolio and video sections on this page show selected wedding, portrait and behind-the-scenes work from Dorpon.

How much does a Dorpon shoot cost?

The price depends on the event, duration and package. Send your event details through the booking form or WhatsApp and Dorpon will discuss the package with you.
